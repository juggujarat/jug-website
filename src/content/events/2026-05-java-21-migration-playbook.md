Gujarat JUG headed back to GIFT City for **"The Java 21 Migration Playbook: Moving Safely from 11 to 21"**, hosted at [**IBM India Software Labs**](https://www.ibm.com/in-en) on the 18th–20th floors of Prestige Fintech. Seats were capped at 60 to keep the session hands-on, and **65 people registered** on Luma.

## Why Java 21, and why now?

Java 21 is the current long-term support (LTS) release, and many teams are still running on Java 11. The session focused on how to move production codebases across safely, without downtime or surprises.

## The playbook

[**Vikas Rajput**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vikasrajputin/), founder of [TechXplore](https://www.techxplore.io), and [**Dhaval Gajjar**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dhavalgajjarin/), System Architect at [Staunchsys](/partners/staunchsys), walked through the upgrade in three parts:

- **The migration roadmap:** spotting compatibility issues, handling deprecated APIs and making the required code changes before they cause outages.
- **Immediate ROI:** the performance gains you get from the upgrade, including **virtual threads**, plus newer built-in security improvements. They also showed performance benchmarks and how the move plays out for financial and banking workloads.
- **Future-proofing:** how the upgrade supports long-term platform stability and developer productivity.

## Our growth story, and a birthday cake

The morning also looked back at the Gujarat JUG journey so far: about a year and a half of meetups, **18 Java meetups** and **2,500+ registrations**. Attendees joined a social media contest, and the morning ended with a **Java birthday celebration and cake cutting** before closing remarks.
