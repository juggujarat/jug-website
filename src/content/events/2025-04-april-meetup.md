On **Saturday, 12 April 2025**, Gujarat JUG met in person at [**7Span**](/partners/7span) on Science City Road, Ahmedabad, for meetup #8. **114 people went** to two talks from people in our own community, from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM.

## Session 1: Reactive Programming in Java

[**Akshay Vadsara**](/people/akshay-vadsara) introduced **reactive programming in Java**: what it is, and how it helps build responsive, non-blocking applications.

## Session 2: The Secret Sauce of Rapid Java Fullstack Development

[**Bharat Ranpariya**](/people/bharat-ranpariya) shared how to move fast when building **Java fullstack** applications, from backend to frontend.

## Thank you

Thanks to our hosts [Dhaval Gajjar](/people/dhaval-gajjar) and [Vikas Rajput](/people/vikas-rajput), and to [7Span](/partners/7span) for hosting the community.
