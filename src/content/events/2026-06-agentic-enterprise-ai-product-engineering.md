On **Saturday, 27 June 2026**, Gujarat JUG gathered at [**Caffix The Tech Cafe**](/partners/caffix) in Thaltej, Ahmedabad, for a hands-on workshop on how AI is changing the way software products are built. **149 people went** to meetup #20, which ran from 10:45 AM to 1:00 PM and ended with snacks and networking.

## The workshop

[**Vinayak Joglekar**](/people/vinayak-joglekar), a serial entrepreneur with 40 years in IT, challenged Java developers to move from writing isolated pieces of code to owning products end to end. The workshop had five parts:

1. **The agentic enterprise transformation:** moving from siloed teams and manual processes to AI agents, virtual teams and value-stream alignment.
2. **The Forward Deployed Product Engineer (FDPE):** owning a product from idea to deployment, translating technical decisions into business value, and leading cross-functional teams.
3. **End-to-end product development with AI agents (live demo):** defining the problem, forming a working group, generating and prioritising ideas with AI, building a playbook and persona, and discovering Jobs-To-Be-Done.
4. **Product requirements:** user stories, acceptance criteria, wireframes, test cases, test data and solution design.
5. **Test-driven development with Claude:** AI-powered TDD, writing tests before implementation, and validating in small iterations.
