Java User Group Gujarat invites you to an online session on **Saturday, 28 November 2026, 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM IST**, with [**Markus Westergren**](/people/markus-westergren), Staff Engineer at Umecon, joining us live from Uppsala, Sweden.

## From Silent Coder to Respected Leader

Markus was once told he was not suited to team work. Decades later, he mentors developers and speaks at international conferences. In this talk he connects his own journey with Java's: from a closed project inside Sun to the open, community-driven ecosystem we know today.

## What you'll take away

- **The turning point** that took him from invisible contributor to a voice in the community
- **How an unexpected hobby** taught him the craft of presenting
- **What good mentors can do** for your career, and what to ask them
- **Why the human side of engineering** (reputation, relationships and storytelling) matters more as AI writes more of the code
- **Concrete steps** to go from consumer to contributor in the Java community

This session is for introverts who avoid the spotlight, and for team leads who want to lift others up.

## About the speaker

[**Markus Westergren**](/people/markus-westergren) is a Staff Engineer with more than 20 years on large Java back-end projects. He helps professional Java developers apply engineering judgment to AI-assisted development, mentors developers and speaks internationally. He writes at [empatheticdev.com](https://empatheticdev.com).
