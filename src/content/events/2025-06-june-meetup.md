On **Saturday, 28 June 2025**, Gujarat JUG met at **York IE in Ambli, Ahmedabad** for the June edition of our monthly meetup. With **116 registrations** on Luma, the morning paired a hands-on technical session with one of the most talked-about career questions of the year.

## Migration engineering as code with OpenRewrite

Upgrading Java versions, moving from Spring Boot 2 to 3, or switching from `javax` to `jakarta` can take teams months when done by hand. [**Vikas Rajput**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vikasrajputin/), Sr. Java Consultant and Gujarat JUG organiser, showed how **OpenRewrite** turns these migrations into code:

- What OpenRewrite *recipes* are and how they safely transform source code using lossless semantic trees
- Running community recipes for Java and Spring Boot upgrades
- Writing custom recipes to automate your organisation's own refactors at scale

## Panel: Becoming an AI-proof engineer

The second half of the morning was a candid panel on how developers can stay relevant as AI changes the way software is built. On the panel:

- [**Jigar Shah**](/people/jigar-shah), Director of Engineering at [DataOrb](https://www.dataorb.ai/)
- [**Dr. Nidhi Arora**](/people/nidhi-arora), Co-Founder & Director-AI at Advicon Solutions
- [**Bhagyesh Radiya**](/people/bhagyesh-radiya), Team Lead at [7Span](https://7span.com/)

They discussed which skills AI makes more valuable rather than less, how teams are actually adopting AI coding tools, and what junior developers should focus on.
