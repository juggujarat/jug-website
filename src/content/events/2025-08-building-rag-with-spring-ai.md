On **Saturday, 30 August 2025**, Gujarat JUG met at [**Staunchsys IT Services**](/partners/staunchsys) in Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, for meetup #12. **110 people went** to learn how Java developers can build AI applications grounded in their own data. The session ran from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM.

## The session: Building RAG with Spring AI

[**Ketan Bhavsar**](/people/ketan-bhavsar), System Architect at [Staunchsys IT Services](/partners/staunchsys) with more than 15 years of experience, showed how **Spring AI** brings large language models into Java applications, and how **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** combines an LLM with a vector database to give answers based on your data. He covered:

- LLMs and **multi-provider support** in Spring AI, including OpenAI, Anthropic and Ollama
- Key Spring AI features: the **Chat Client API**, **prompt templates** and **structured outputs**
- **Function calling**
- **RAG with vector databases**
- A **live demo** putting it all together
