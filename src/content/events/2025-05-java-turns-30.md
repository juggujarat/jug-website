On **Saturday, 24 May 2025**, a day after Java turned **30 years old**, and Gujarat JUG celebrated in style. The special edition, **"Java Turns 30: Celebrating Three Decades of Innovation"**, took place at **smartSense Consulting Solutions in GIFT One, GIFT City, Gandhinagar**, with **124 people registering** on Luma.

## From legacy to legendary

The main session, **"From Legacy to Legendary: Unleashing Business Agility Through ERP Microservices"** by [**Niraj Salot**](/people/niraj-salot), looked at how organisations break large ERP monoliths into microservices to move faster, and the role Java continues to play in that journey after three decades.

## Open mic: share your Java story

Instead of a second talk, the floor was opened to the community. In the **open mic**, attendees shared how they started with Java, the projects that shaped them, and what keeps them on the JVM 30 years on.

## Cake, community and group photos

The celebration ended the only way a birthday should: with **cake cutting**, refreshments, group photos and a long community hangout.

## Thank you, smartSense

Thanks to [**smartSense Consulting Solutions**](https://www.smartsensesolutions.com/) for hosting us at GIFT City and helping make Java's 30th birthday one to remember.
