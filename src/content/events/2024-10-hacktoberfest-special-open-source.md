On **Sunday, 20 October 2024**, Java User Group Gujarat went online for a **Hacktoberfest special**, meetup #4 in our series. The session ran on Zoom and **61 people joined**, including community regulars such as [Bharat Ranpariya](/people/bharat-ranpariya).

## Why a Hacktoberfest special?

Every October, Hacktoberfest invites developers around the world to contribute to open source. Many Java developers want to take part but are not sure where to start. This session was built for exactly them: people new to open source, or who wanted to get more involved, so they could contribute with confidence during the month.

## What we covered

[**Rohan Kumar**](/people/rohan-kumar), Software Developer at Red Hat, led the session:

- What open source is, with a short history
- The benefits of contributing, for your skills and your career
- A step-by-step guide to a first contribution
- A checklist to run through before you contribute
- Examples of open source Java projects worth exploring
- Common mistakes in a first pull request, and how to avoid them
- A **live demo** of making a contribution from start to finish
