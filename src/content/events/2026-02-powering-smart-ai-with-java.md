On **Saturday, 21 February 2026**, Gujarat JUG's **"Powering Smart AI with Java"** meetup took place at **Royal Technosoft, Navrangpura, Ahmedabad**. The event sold out, with **110 people registering** on Luma for a deep dive into modern AI search built with Java.

## Modern search with vector databases and RAG

The main session, **"Powering Smart AI: Modern Search with Vector Databases & RAG with Java"**, was delivered by [**Bharat Ranpariya**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bharat-ranpariya/), Technical Engineering Manager at [DataOrb](https://www.dataorb.ai/), and [**Aniket Datt**](/people/aniket-datt), Senior Software Engineer at [Thomson Reuters](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/). Attendees learned:

- How Java libraries turn text into **embeddings** (vectors)
- How **open-source LLMs** can lower AI infrastructure costs
- Why **vector databases** are the backbone of AI-powered search
- How **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** grounds LLMs in enterprise knowledge
- Practical use cases and architecture patterns for building reliable AI applications

## A morning of learning and networking

The meetup opened with high tea and check-in, followed by a welcome note from the Gujarat JUG team, the main session, a short slot from our venue partner, and a closing note. It ended with a networking lunch.
