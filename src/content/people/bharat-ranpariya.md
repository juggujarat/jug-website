Bharat Ranpariya is a co-organizer of Gujarat JUG and a Technical Engineering Manager at DataOrb. Leading engineering teams showed him how much developers benefit from mentors, peers and honest technical discussion, and he joined Gujarat JUG to give Java developers in Gujarat a place to find all three.

Bharat cares deeply about building a strong and vibrant Java community, and he is passionate about helping developers grow their skills and careers.
