Dhaval Gajjar is a co-organizer of Gujarat JUG and a System Architect at Staunchsys IT Services. Designing Java systems day to day, he knows how much developers gain from learning together, and he was excited to help build the platform Gujarat's Java developers never had.

Dhaval is deeply invested in building a strong and vibrant Java community, and he is passionate about helping developers, from students to seasoned engineers, sharpen their skills through real-world talks and conversations.
