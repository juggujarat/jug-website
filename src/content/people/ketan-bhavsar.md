Ketan Bhavsar is a System Architect at Staunchsys IT Services in Ahmedabad, with more than 15 years of experience designing and leading large-scale Java software across finance, healthcare, trading, sports technology and enterprise systems. He specialises in Java, Spring Boot, microservices, Angular and Node.js.

He has led real-time trading engines, enterprise invoice management systems, Keycloak single sign-on, financial analytics platforms and real-time sports trading systems for clients in the UK, US and India, with a focus on scalable, event-driven, high-frequency architectures. He is now exploring applied AI and prompt engineering, and mentors teams through Agile and Scrum.

At Java User Group Gujarat, Ketan presented **"Building RAG with Spring AI"** in August 2025.
