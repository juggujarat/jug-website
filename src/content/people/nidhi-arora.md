Dr. Nidhi Arora is Co-Founder & Director-AI at Advicon Solutions in Ahmedabad and one of Gujarat's best-known voices in Artificial Intelligence. A Microsoft Certified Associate with more than two decades in AI and Machine Learning research and industry, she works across Generative AI, computer vision, natural language processing, predictive analytics, workflow automation and business intelligence, and has designed AI solutions for clients around the world.

A former director at GTU and former Chief AI Officer, Dr. Arora has published more than 70 research papers and the book *Soft Computing Methodologies for Bankruptcy Prediction*, with chapters published by Springer, Elsevier, IGI Global and Taylor & Francis. She serves on editorial and reviewer boards of international journals, is a Ph.D. co-guide and examiner, and sits on the Board of Studies at Indus University.

As a Google Developer Educator and keynote speaker, she has spoken at universities across Gujarat, at IIT Bombay, at Infosys, and at industry events including ISHRAE, ASHRAE and FSAI. In April 2025 she featured on the cover of *Insights Success* magazine as an Ethical AI Champion. At Java User Group Gujarat she joined the **"Becoming an AI-Proof Engineer"** panel in June 2025.
