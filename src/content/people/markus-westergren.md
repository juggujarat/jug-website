Markus Westergren is a Staff Engineer at Umecon, based in Uppsala, Sweden, with more than 20 years of experience on large Java back-end projects. He helps professional Java developers apply sound engineering judgment to AI-assisted development, mentors developers and speaks at international conferences. He writes at [empatheticdev.com](https://empatheticdev.com).

Early in his career Markus was told he was not suited to team work. At Java User Group Gujarat he presents **"From Silent Coder to Respected Leader"** in November 2026, an online session on how he, and Java itself, grew from closed and quiet beginnings into open, community-driven voices.
