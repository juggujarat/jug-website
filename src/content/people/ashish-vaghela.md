Ashish Vaghela is a Software Crafter at Nelkinda Software Craft and a full-stack engineer who believes convenience should never come at the cost of understanding what is actually running in production. He has contributed to open source projects big and small, and cares deeply about code quality, disciplined testing and software that holds up under real-world load.

He runs WeDoTDD, a community for developers who take Test-Driven Development and software craftsmanship seriously, and mentors engineers and organises meetups. At Java User Group Gujarat he presented **"Jugaad vs JFR: What Your Java Libraries Are Really Costing You"** in September 2026.
