Vikas Rajput is a co-organizer of Gujarat JUG and the founder of TechXplore. Having built his career around Java, he saw that Gujarat had thousands of Java developers but no dedicated platform where they could meet, learn from each other and share what they were building. Filling that gap is what got him excited about starting Gujarat JUG.

Vikas has a deep interest in building a strong and vibrant Java community in Gujarat, and a real passion for helping developers grow, whether that means giving a first-time speaker a stage or connecting local talent with the global Java ecosystem.
